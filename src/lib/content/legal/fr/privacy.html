Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Funniversaries collects, why, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. We try to collect as little as possible: an email address, the dates you claim, and the technical data needed to run the site.

1. Who we are

The service at funniversaries.com (the "Service") is operated by 1100Labs (eleven hundred labs), based in France ("we", "us"). For the personal data described below, we are the data controller, except for payment data (see section 3).

Contact for any privacy question or request: 1100labs@proton.me

2. What data we collect

  • Email address.

    Received from our payment provider when you buy, and used to deliver your purchase and to recover it later.

  • Purchase information.

    Order ID, payment status, license key and the plan you bought. We never see or store your payment card details.

  • The dates you claim.

    When you buy a plan and claim a date (for example a birthday, a relationship date or a child’s date of birth), we store that date and link it to your purchase so that you can get it back. Please only enter dates you are comfortable storing with us, and only dates you are entitled to use.

  • Technical data.

    IP address, timestamps, browser information and request or error logs, processed by our infrastructure provider to deliver and secure the Service.

  • Messages you send us.

    The content of emails you send to our contact address.

We do not ask for your name, address or phone number, and we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking.

4. Who receives your data

We share data only with the providers that are needed to run the Service:

  • Lemon Squeezy (payments).

    Lemon Squeezy acts as the seller and merchant of record. It collects and processes your payment details and handles VAT under its own responsibility and its own privacy policy. We receive your email address, payment status, order ID and license key. See Lemon Squeezy’s Privacy Policy.

  • Cloudflare (hosting and storage).

    We use Cloudflare Pages to host the website, Cloudflare Workers to run the service, and Cloudflare D1 as the database that stores email addresses, purchase records, license information and claimed dates. Cloudflare acts as our processor. Data stored in D1 is encrypted at rest by Cloudflare. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

  • Resend (email delivery).

    Resend sends the emails needed to recover your purchase and license. It receives your email address and the content of those messages, and acts as our processor. See Resend’s Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, for example in response to a valid request from a court or public authority, or to protect our rights in a legal claim. If the business is ever sold or transferred, your data may be transferred with it and this policy will continue to apply or you will be informed of any change.

5. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Our providers are based in, or may process data in, the United States and other countries outside the EEA. Where this happens, the transfer relies on an adequacy decision (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for certified providers) or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, together with supplementary measures where needed. You can contact us to get more information about the safeguards that apply.

6. Cookies and local storage

We keep this to the strict minimum, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

  • Session cookie (strictly necessary).

    When you claim a purchase or verify your email, we set a cookie containing a random token that identifies your session, so that you can see the dates you bought. Only a hashed form of the token is stored on our side. This cookie is required to provide the feature you ask for.

  • Language preference (local storage).

    Your browser may store your chosen language so that dates and text are displayed in your format. It stays on your device and is not sent to us for tracking.

  • Analytics.

    We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure traffic. It does not use cookies or local storage for analytics.

Because we only use strictly necessary cookies and storage, we do not show a cookie consent banner. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but you will not be able to access your purchased dates without the session cookie.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Purchase, order and invoice records: 10 years, as required by French accounting and commercial law.

  • Email address, license and claimed dates: for as long as your purchase may need to be recovered, since that is the purpose of the data. You can ask us to delete them earlier (see section 8); you will then lose the ability to recover your dates, and we will keep only the records we must legally retain.

  • Messages you send us: up to 24 months after our last exchange.

  • Technical logs: for the shortest period needed for security and troubleshooting, in line with our infrastructure provider’s retention settings.

Once these periods end, data is deleted or anonymized. Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited time before being overwritten.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased, subject to our legal retention duties;
  • restrict the processing of your data in certain cases;
  • receive the data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used format (data portability);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your situation;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it (this does not affect earlier processing).

To exercise any of these rights, email 1100labs@proton.me. We will answer within one month. We may ask you to verify your identity, typically by confirming the email address used for your purchase, so that your data is not disclosed to someone else.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed session tokens, and access limited to what the Service needs. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the competent authority as required by law.

10. Children

The Service is not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

You may enter a child’s date (for example a baby’s date of birth) to see its milestones. If you do, you confirm you are entitled to do so, for example as a parent or guardian.

11. Links to other websites

The Service may link to third-party websites. We do not control them and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please read their policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the "Last updated" date above. For material changes, we will also inform customers by email or by a notice on the Service before the change takes effect.
